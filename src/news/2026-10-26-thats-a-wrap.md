Well, that’s a wrap. The curtain is closed, the castle has been dismantled, and the capes and wands are packed away.

Theatre 17 and the Cast & Creative Team of Puffs would like to thank everyone who came out and supported us. Hearing people say “Oh, that was so funny!” or “You guys were awesome!” is the ultimate payoff for all the work this team has put in over the las six months.

This project started out as just a fun show and evolved into so much more for all of us involved. Puffs is about finding your people, and we all did just that.

We will be collecting and posting photos from the show in the next couple weeks.

We had a blast transporting you to our magical land.

Third or Nothing!
